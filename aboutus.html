About Us

Pastor Dismus Mutuku

A life dedicated to spreading the Gospel, healing, and empowering others through the Holy Spirit.

Pastor Dismus Mutuku has been at the forefront of missions, from cities to villages, with a deep burden to see people receive the Kingdom of God, be set free, and filled with the Holy Spirit. With years of experience in ministry, Pastor Dismus has organized and led numerous Missions, Conferences, and Revival Meetings, reaching countless individuals and raising up ministers who continue the work of evangelism.

His ministry, Hope For Life Ministry, is based in Nairobi, where he focuses on the strength that comes from biblical truths. These truths enhance the revelation of God's Kingdom through the graces given by the Holy Spirit. Pastor Dismus emphasizes the importance of practicing Faith, Prayer, and the Word of God as the foundation of spiritual growth and service to others.

Rev. Oliver Ingila

Mark 16:15 "And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature."

Rev. Oliver Ingila is a passionate servant of God and a devoted preacher whose ministry is firmly anchored in the Great Commission as declared in Mark 16:15. Guided by this divine mandate, Rev. Oliver has dedicated his life to spreading the transformative message of the Gospel to people across all walks of life.

With a heart for missions and evangelism, Rev. Oliver has traveled extensively, reaching communities near and far with the love of Christ. His ministry is marked by a relentless commitment to sharing the Good News, bringing hope to the hopeless, and empowering believers to live out their faith boldly.

Pastor Lamarck Oyath

Mathew 4:23 "And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people."

Pastor Lamarck Oyath is a visionary leader, dynamic speaker, and dedicated servant of God with a deep passion for inspiring lives and empowering communities. Pastor Lamarck has devoted himself to sharing the transformative message of faith, hope, and love.

Pastor Lamarck has been instrumental in fostering a welcoming and vibrant faith community. Through his heartfelt sermons, Pastor Lamarck delivers powerful messages that connect biblical truths to everyday life, encouraging individuals to grow spiritually, overcome challenges, and live purpose-filled lives.

Minister Rose Wanjiru Kangethe

1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 "Rejoice always, pray continually, give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus."

Minister Rose Wanjiru Kang’ethe is a passionate and dedicated servant of God, overseeing the Intercessory Ministry and Sunday School in her church. Her role is deeply rooted in biblical principles, with a clear focus on nurturing spiritual growth and guiding others in their faith journey.

In her capacity as the leader of the Intercessory Ministry, Minister Kang’ethe exemplifies the power of prayer. Drawing inspiration from scriptures like 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18, she emphasizes the importance of rejoicing always, praying without ceasing, and giving thanks in all circumstances. She organizes prayer sessions for individuals, families, and the wider community, mentoring others to become steadfast in prayer and faith. Her leadership fosters a spirit of unity and transformation within the congregation.

Pastor George Ngugi

Matthew 20:26-28 "Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant."

Pastor George Ngugi is a committed and dedicated servant of God, overseeing the Ministry of Logistics at his church. His role is deeply aligned with biblical principles, with a strong emphasis on serving others and ensuring the smooth operation of church activities in support of spiritual growth and outreach.

As the leader of the Ministry of Logistics, Pastor Ngugi exemplifies the biblical call to serve. He is passionate about organizing and coordinating church events and resources in a way that facilitates ministry and strengthens the congregation. Pastor Ngugi’s leadership is marked by a spirit of selfless service, ensuring that every aspect of the church’s operations is carried out with excellence, providing support to both the church leadership and the community.
