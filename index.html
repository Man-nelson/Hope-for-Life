Vision

To reach out for lost souls and lead them to the saving grace of Jesus Christ. We are dedicated to preaching and teaching the Word of God with power and truth, guiding individuals to a deeper relationship with Him.

Mission

To reach the lost with the life-changing message of Jesus Christ. We are committed to equipping and training believers in their faith, helping them grow spiritually and become strong disciples. Through the power of the Holy Spirit, we seek to empower the Body of Christ to live out their calling with boldness and purpose.
